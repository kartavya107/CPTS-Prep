# CPTS Prep

## About me

I am currently a graduate student at PACE University - NYC pursuing an MS in Cybersecurity. I aspire to be a highly competent Penetration Tester and I intend to document my learning process openly as much as I can.

## What is HackTheBox & CPTS

- **HackTheBox (HTB):** An online platform of realistic penetration-testing labs and CTF-style machines used to practice offensive security skills along with defensive security skills as well. However, since I want to be a competent Penetration Tester, I would be majorly focussing on offensive security skills. HTB hosts vulnerable machines, challenges, and learning paths that simulate real-world attack scenarios in their lab.
- **CPTS (Certified Penetration Testing Specialist):** A professional certification that validates practical penetration-testing skills and methodology. Preparing for CPTS involves hands-on labs, writeups, and consistent practice across multiple domains (network, web, privilege escalation, etc.).

## Purpose & Motivation

I document my CPTS preparation to record the labs I complete while studying for the CPTS certification. This repository is a chronological record of the boxes I solve, the techniques I practice, and the lessons I learn. I maintain these writeups primarily to support my own learning and growth as I prepare for the CPTS exam. Writing reports helps me build discipline, clarify my thinking, improve my technical intuition and report-writing, and track progress over time. Each writeup is a concise artifact I can review later — a record of what worked, what to revisit, and a small pat on the back after finishing a box.

I follow IppSec's CPTS Prep series and frequently consult his walkthroughs when I get stuck. Even after I comfortably solve a box start-to-finish, I still watch IppSec's walkthroughs as they are concise and concepts & thought process is explained in depth — I use them as a learning resource to compare approaches and fill gaps in understanding.
